Backend automation between LAXMI Lamitube's software and this ERP — no manual entry

Connected
Both databases sit with us, so the exchange runs in the backend LAXMI Lamitube Pvt. Ltd. is a separate company with its own GST and its own printing software. Because we hold both databases, a scheduled backend job reads printing job data straight from their tables and writes it against the matching work order here. Nobody re-keys anything on either side — the documents that carry legal weight (challan, job work invoice) are still raised normally.
