Purchase Requisition

Raise a requisition directly, or let the work order raise it — also called an indent

Two ways a requisition is created, one place it gets approved Stores, maintenance or purchase can raise one directly from this screen. A Production Work Order also raises one automatically for its material shortfall, under its own authority. Either way the requisition then moves to the Approvals panel in the top bar — the purchase order stays locked until it is cleared there.

Where each requisition goes next

1 Raised — manually here, or automatically by a work order
2 Items, quantity and required-by date recorded
3 Sits in the Approvals panel in the top bar
4 Approved — purchase order is unlocked for those lines
5 Rejected — goes back to whoever raised it, with a remark

Nothing is approved on this screen. Approval for every document type — requisition, purchase order, quotation, sales order, quality disposition and CAPA — happens in one place, so an approver has a single queue rather than six screens.
